FrameMatch Concept demo

Near-duplicate video detection — how the pipeline behaves

The real tool fingerprints video frames and compares them to find re-encoded, trimmed, or resized copies. It can't run OpenCV in a browser, so this is a faithful model of the two decisions that drive it: how often to sample frames, and how strict a match has to be. A synthetic "edited clip" is compared against its "source" below — move the sliders and watch precision and cost trade against each other.

Model, not the engine: fingerprints here are stand-ins so the demo runs instantly and offline. The real pipeline computes perceptual hashes and ORB descriptors with OpenCV and matches them across CPU cores. The trade-off you're moving — recall versus cost, strictness versus robustness — is the real one.
