Cost & Revenue vs Stage Length September 2026 • 144 lanes

Does a longer haul earn its cost? Each bubble is a lane, positioned by its stage length (great-circle reference miles) and sized by pounds. Cost/lb is booked TLM expense at lane grain. Revenue/lb is station revenue allocated to lanes by each lane's share of its origin's pounds — station totals tie exactly, but an individual lane's revenue is an estimate, not a booked figure.

Click a series in the legend to show or hide it. Trend lines are pounds-weighted, so a single small lane cannot tilt them.

Lanes above the zero line generate more trucking-enabled revenue than they cost to run; lanes below it do not. Revenue is allocated, so read this as directional rather than as a booked lane margin.
